How it works

Three steps. Then they start.

  1. 01

    You call or send the form

    Give us the start date, the roles, and the headcount.

  2. 02

    We crew up

    Screened, trained workers are assigned, onboarded, and scheduled.

  3. 03

    They start

    We run payroll, scheduling, WCB, and compliance.

With us, a full crew is a matter of days. Hiring direct usually takes weeks. Screening, onboarding, and payroll are already done on our side. The only paperwork on yours is a short non-solicitation agreement.

  1. 01 Start date
  2. 02 Crew and headcount
  3. 03 Farm name
  4. 04 Site address

What stays with us

You run the harvest. We run the employment.

  • $5 million liability

    Insurance in place, and you named as additional insured.

  • PEI standards

    Workers scheduled under Prince Edward Island employment standards.

  • No payroll admin

    We remit HST, CPP, EI, and tax. Same-day cover when someone does not show, subject to availability.

Why farms call

  • 01

    Days, not weeks

    A crew can be on site within days of the call.
