Compliance & safety

Compliance and safety, handled on our side.

Labour rules and the standards a site has to meet. We run payroll, scheduling, WCB, and compliance, and workers complete their safety and compliance training through the UnScramble worker app.

  • Training in the app

What stays with us

You run the harvest. We run the employment.

  • $5 million liability

    Insurance in place, and you named as additional insured.

  • PEI standards

    Workers scheduled under Prince Edward Island employment standards.

  • No payroll admin

    We remit HST, CPP, EI, and tax. Same-day cover when someone does not show, subject to availability.

UnScramble worker app

Safety training, done in the app.

Employees complete their safety and compliance training through the UnScramble worker app. Each person gets the items for their job role: a short module to read, then a quiz.

The training items

  • WHMIS 2015

    Reading labels and Safety Data Sheets, pictograms and signal words. Product- and site-specific WHMIS is given at the site.

  • PEI worker rights and responsibilities

    The right to know about hazards, to take part in health and safety, and to refuse unsafe work.

  • Site safety orientation

    Farm roles take the farm orientation. Other client sites take general site safety plus a short add-on for construction, bakery, painting or cleaning work.

  • Forklift / machinery operator

    For forklift and machinery roles, together with the operator certificate.

  • Food safety / GMP hygiene

    For graders and packers.

  • Harassment and violence prevention

    What is not accepted, how to report it, and confidentiality.

  • Incident reporting

    Report every injury, near miss and hazard right away.

  • First aid

    For designated first aiders: a current first aid certificate on file.

  • Workers are asked to finish every item for their role before their first shift.
  • Pass mark is 80%. Questions and answers are shuffled on every attempt, and after each attempt the worker sees the correct answers with a short explanation. Retakes are allowed right away.
  • Every attempt is recorded with the date, time and score. Passed items renew every 12 months, with reminders before they are due.
  • The office keeps a training matrix showing each worker's status for each item.

Every UnScramble employee completes their safety training in the app before starting work. When training is updated, employees are asked to complete the new version in the app.

On site

The site walk-through still happens at the site.

Site-specific parts, such as the products used, the farm walk-through and food safety rules, are given at the site by the supervisor.

  • Site orientation first

    Where a site needs its own orientation, it is recorded in the app before that worker clocks in there.

  • PEI standards

    Workers scheduled under Prince Edward Island employment standards.

  • Your safety requirements

    Tell us your site's safety requirements and we'll match workers who meet them.

Records

Paperwork kept in one place.

  • Workers accept the worker acknowledgement and the privacy notice in the app.
  • Proof of eligibility to work in Canada is kept on file, with permit expiry dates tracked.
  • Licences and certificates for the role (for example driver and forklift) are uploaded and tracked to their expiry dates.
  • We remit HST, CPP, EI, and tax, and run payroll, scheduling and WCB.
